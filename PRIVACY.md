# Privacy Policy

**S&S Clearer for Amazon** is committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

This extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit any user data.

Specifically, we do not collect:
- Personally identifiable information
- Browsing history
- Website content
- Authentication information
- Financial information
- Health information
- Location data
- User activity or behavior data

## How the Extension Works

When you open the extension on your Amazon Subscribe & Save list, it:
1. Reads the product names, images and subscription IDs shown on that page, only to
   display them in the popup so you can choose what to cancel
2. After you confirm, clicks Amazon's own Edit → Cancel subscription → Confirm buttons for
   each subscription you selected
3. Keeps the list of selected subscriptions and a progress count in Chrome's local
   extension storage while the run is in progress, so it can continue after each page
   reload. This is overwritten by the next run and removed when you uninstall.

All operations happen locally in your browser. No data is sent to any external servers.

## Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions solely to perform its core function:

- **storage**: To remember an in-progress run locally on your computer
- **Access to www.amazon.com**: To read your subscription list and click Amazon's cancel
  buttons for the subscriptions you selected. The extension does nothing on any other site.

These permissions are used exclusively for cancelling the subscriptions you choose and
nothing else.

## Third Parties

This extension does not:
- Share data with third parties
- Use analytics or tracking
- Include advertisements
- Make network requests of its own

S&S Clearer is not affiliated with Amazon. Your use of Amazon is governed by Amazon's own
privacy notice.
